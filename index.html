Before you pay, you probably want to know

The questions we get on WhatsApp most often.

Is this legit?

You pay to our Lipa na M-Pesa Till and get a confirmation straight away. Message us first, ask anything, and look through our Instagram for past trips.

What am I really paying for?

Each trip lists what's included and what isn't. The price you see is the price you pay, apart from your own food, drinks and spending where stated.

I'm coming solo. Is that weird?

No. Most of our travellers come alone. We run games on the road so you're chatting before we reach the first stop.

Is it safe, especially for women?

You get the pickup point, the trip lead's contact and the full plan before the day. Nobody is left behind or left to find their own way.

More questions

Can I pay everything at once?

Yes. The deposit locks your seat, and you can pay the full amount whenever you like. The balance is due before departure.

What if I can't make it?

Tell us on WhatsApp before you pay and we'll send the cancellation and seat-transfer terms for that trip, so you know the rules up front.

Where do we meet in Nairobi?

The pickup point and time are sent to you after your deposit is confirmed, and you can ask us before you book.

What if it rains or plans change?

We message the whole group on WhatsApp as soon as anything changes, with the new plan or date.

Can I come solo?

Yes. Most of the group comes solo and you meet everyone on the way.
